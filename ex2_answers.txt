1. What happens when the number of hidden nodes increase?
Accuracy jumps sharply from 1–2 hidden units (67%) to 3+ hidden units (90%), then stays roughly flat around 90–91% from 3 all the way up to 50 hidden units — it doesn't keep climbing indefinitely.

2. Can you explain the pattern?
With only 1–2 hidden units, the network doesn't have enough capacity to model the decision boundary of this dataset, so it underfits and gets stuck around 67%. Once you hit 3+ units, there's enough capacity to fit the data well, and accuracy plateaus around 90–91% — adding more units beyond that (20, 50) doesn't help much because the extra capacity isn't needed for this problem, and can even risk overfitting on a small dataset rather than improving generalization.
